CruiseLAN Support

Play Chess, Checkers, Connect Four, Tic-Tac-Toe, Color Match, Spades, and Tonk with family and friends: online with internet, with people nearby without it, or against the computer.

Contact and safety

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL. We read every message and safety report and respond as quickly as we can, usually within two days.

If someone makes you feel unsafe, tell a parent or a crew member right away. In a game, tap Report (top of the game screen) to report a player. The host of a game can remove a player from the lobby, and you can leave any game at any time.

Playing

  • Quick Play: open a game and tap Quick Play to join a game that's waiting for players, or start one anyone can join.
  • With friends: tap Host a New Game, choose Private, and share the code or invite link.
  • On your own: Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four, Checkers, and Chess have Play the Computer. It works with no internet.
  • No internet? Games are hosted on a phone instead. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, open CruiseLAN on each phone, and allow Local Network when asked. Open games nearby appear in each game's list.

Nobody nearby shows up?

  • Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › CruiseLAN is on.
  • Move closer: nearby connections reach people close by, not across a whole ship.
  • Some ship and hotel Wi-Fi networks block phones from seeing each other. In CruiseLAN, Profile › Troubleshooting explains what's working and runs a network test.

Notifications

Allow Notifications to hear when it's your turn while you're in another app. Online games notify you through Apple's push service. Games hosted nearby nudge your phone over Bluetooth, which needs Bluetooth on and CruiseLAN still running in the background.

Privacy

No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Online games keep only their moves and nicknames and are deleted automatically. Read the Privacy Policy.
