CruiseLAN Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026 (version 2.0)

CruiseLAN lets people play games together: online when the internet is available, with people nearby when it isn't, or against the computer on your own phone. It is designed to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

CruiseLAN has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking, and we never sell or share personal information. The only data that reaches us is what our online game service needs to host an online game, described below. Games hosted nearby and games against the computer never reach us.

Online games

When the internet is available, games are hosted by our online game service (run on Cloudflare) instead of on a phone, so friends can play from anywhere and a game survives a phone dropping out. For each online game the service keeps:

  • the game and its moves, including the cards dealt, so it can enforce the rules and show each player only their own cards;
  • the nickname you play under, shown to the other players in that game;
  • a random secret that lets your phone rejoin its seat (we store only a scrambled form of it);
  • if you allow notifications, your phone's push address (a random token from Apple), so the game can tell you when someone joins, the game starts, or it's your turn while CruiseLAN is closed. Notifications contain no player names.

Open and private games. Games are open by default: while an open game waits for players, the game's name, your nickname and how many seats are taken appear in a list that everyone using CruiseLAN can see, so they can join. It leaves the list when it fills up, starts, or ends. A private game is never listed; only people you give its code to can join. The host of a game can remove a player before it starts, and anyone can report a player.

There's no account and nothing that identifies you or links your games together. Your phone's internet address is seen by the service while you're connected, as with any website, and is used only to limit abuse such as guessing invite codes or flooding the open-games list. It isn't stored with your games; an open game keeps only a scrambled tag made from it with a secret key, which can't be turned back into the address, and is deleted with the game. Cloudflare's request logs, which we use only to fix problems, are kept for a few days.

Retention and deletion. Each online game, with everything above, is deleted automatically: 24 hours after creation if it never starts, 7 days after the last move, or 30 days after it ends. Leaving a game removes you from it. To have a game deleted sooner, email us its code at the address below. You can turn online games off in Profile; then every game is hosted on a phone nearby and nothing reaches us.

What stays on your device

  • Your nickname, the list of people you've blocked, and devices you've recently connected with (forgotten after 24 hours) are saved on your phone so the app works between launches.
  • So turn notifications keep working if iOS closes CruiseLAN in the background, your phone keeps its temporary CruiseLAN ID, the nicknames of people you recently played with nearby, and the Bluetooth keys shared with them in the iPhone Keychain, on this device only, for up to 12 hours after you last used the app.
  • Nearby games and games against the computer are kept only in memory and are erased when you quit the app.
  • If you're in an online game, your phone keeps that game's invite code and seat secret in the iPhone Keychain, on this device only, so it can go back to the game if iOS closes CruiseLAN. It's removed when the game ends or you leave.
  • Connection measurements shown in Troubleshooting and Insights (counts and timings) stay on your phone. You can delete them in Insights.

What other players can see

In any game, the other players see your nickname, whether you're connected, and your moves. They never see your cards. There's no chat.

What nearby people can see

When there's no internet, an open game you host on your phone is announced to CruiseLAN users nearby with your nickname, so anyone nearby can join it. A private one is only sent to the people you invite. So people can find you, your phone shares your nickname and a short tag (like #A1B2) with CruiseLAN users nearby while CruiseLAN is open. Game moves travel between phones over encrypted Wi-Fi or Bluetooth links; other CruiseLAN phones in between may pass them along but can't read them. Nothing passes through us.

While CruiseLAN is in the background or your phone is locked, your phone advertises a short-lived Bluetooth identifier so nearby players in your game can tell it it's your turn or deliver a move. These Bluetooth messages are encrypted with a key only your phone and theirs share. The identifier changes every time the app starts.

Safety reports

If you report a player, CruiseLAN opens an email in the Mail app addressed to us, containing the reason, the player's nickname, and (for an online game) the game's code. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. We use reports only to respond to safety concerns and improve the safety filter, and we delete them when they're resolved.

Children

CruiseLAN is suitable for families. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: online games use a nickname and moves only, with no account, and are deleted automatically. An on-device filter blocks inappropriate words and personal details such as phone numbers, emails, links, and cabin numbers in nicknames.

Permissions

  • Local Network: to find and connect to nearby phones running CruiseLAN, and, only if you run the network test in Troubleshooting, to check whether the Wi-Fi lets devices see each other.
  • Bluetooth: to connect nearby and to send and receive turn nudges.
  • Notifications: to tell you when it's your turn or someone joins your game.
  • Camera (optional): only to scan a friend's connect code. No photos or video are taken or stored.
  • Location (optional, only if you tap "Show name" in Troubleshooting): iOS requires it before an app can read the name of the Wi-Fi network you're on. CruiseLAN uses it only to show that name on your screen. It never records, stores, or shares your location or the network name.
  • Internet (no permission prompt): for online games, only while you're in one or when CruiseLAN checks whether online games are available.

Third parties

CruiseLAN contains no third-party SDKs. The online game service runs on Cloudflare, which processes data only to run the service for us, and push notifications are delivered by Apple. Neither may use this data for anything else.
