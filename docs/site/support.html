CruiseLAN Support

Offline chat and games with people nearby, with no internet needed.

Contact and safety

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL. We read every safety report and respond as quickly as we can, usually within two days of the message reaching us. Mail sends it once your phone is back online.

If someone makes you feel unsafe, tell a parent or a crew member right away. In the app, touch and hold any message to Report or Block the sender.

Getting connected

  1. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on both phones. You don't have to join a network.
  2. Open CruiseLAN on both phones and allow Local Network when asked.
  3. Tap People (top right of Chat), tap the other person, and Invite. They tap Accept.

Nobody shows up?

  • Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › CruiseLAN is on.
  • Move closer. CruiseLAN reaches people nearby, not across the whole ship.
  • Some ship and hotel Wi-Fi networks block phones from seeing each other. Try turning Wi-Fi on but not joining any network.

Notifications and nudges

Allow Notifications and Bluetooth to hear about new messages and your turn while you're in another app. Nudges work within Bluetooth range, and only while CruiseLAN is still running in the background. If you swipe it away in the app switcher, open it again to reconnect.

Privacy

No accounts, servers, ads, or tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.
